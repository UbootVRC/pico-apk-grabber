**Downloads**
- **PicoApkGrabber.exe** (Windows): download APKs of apps your PICO account owns to your PC, and optionally install them on a PICO 4 Enterprise over USB, including extra data (OBB) files.
- **PicoApkGrabber.apk** (Android): sideload once onto the PICO 4 Enterprise (or a phone), sign in with your consumer PICO account, then install apps you own right on the headset.

**New in this version:** the Windows app and **Download + install on headset (USB)**.

**Status: untested.** Not yet tried with PICO's live servers, on a real headset, or with a real USB install. Please report problems with the text from the app's log.

The .exe isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about it: click **More info → Run anyway**.
